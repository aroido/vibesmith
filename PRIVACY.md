# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: February 18, 2026  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

VibeSmith ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use VibeSmith.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Local Data (Free Tier)

VibeSmith is a **local-first application**. In the Free Tier:

- ✅ **All data stays on your device**
- ✅ No data transmitted to our servers
- ✅ No account creation required
- ✅ No personal information collected

**Local data includes:**
- Project paths and configurations
- AI agent components (Skills, Agents, Commands, Hooks, Rules)
- User preferences and settings
- Application state and cache

**Storage location:**
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/VibeSmith`
- Windows: `%APPDATA%/VibeSmith`
- Linux: `~/.config/vibesmith`

### 1.2 Cloud Data (Pro/Team Tiers)

When you upgrade to Pro or Team tiers:

- 📧 **Email address** (for account creation and communication)
- 💳 **Payment information** (processed by Stripe, not stored by us)
- ☁️ **Synced data** (your AI components, if you enable cloud sync)
- 📊 **Usage analytics** (anonymous, aggregated statistics)
- 🐛 **Crash reports** (if you opt in)

### 1.3 Automatically Collected Information

- **Application version** and **OS information**
- **Error logs** (stored locally, sent only if you opt in)
- **Performance metrics** (anonymous, if you opt in)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Free Tier

- Provide local functionality only
- No data transmission or collection

### 2.2 Pro/Team Tiers

- **Account Management**: Create and manage your account
- **Cloud Sync**: Synchronize your components across devices
- **Billing**: Process payments (via Stripe)
- **Support**: Respond to your support requests
- **Product Improvement**: Analyze usage patterns (anonymous)
- **Communication**: Send service updates and newsletters (opt-in)

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **NOT** sell your personal information.

We may share your data only in these cases:

### 3.1 Service Providers

- **Stripe**: Payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
- **AWS/Cloudflare**: Cloud infrastructure and CDN
- **Sentry**: Error monitoring (if you opt in)

All service providers are bound by data protection agreements.

### 3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to:
- Comply with legal processes
- Protect our rights and property
- Prevent fraud or security threats
- Protect user safety

### 3.3 Business Transfers

If VibeSmith is acquired or merged, your information may be transferred to the new owner. You will be notified of any such change.

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- 🔒 **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
- 🔐 **Access Control**: Strict employee access policies
- 🛡️ **Security Audits**: Regular security assessments
- 🔥 **Firewall**: DDoS protection and WAF (Cloudflare)

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Use VibeSmith at your own risk.

---

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Free Tier

- Data stored locally indefinitely (until you delete it)

### 5.2 Pro/Team Tiers

- **Active accounts**: Data retained as long as account is active
- **Inactive accounts**: Data deleted after 90 days of inactivity (with notice)
- **Deleted accounts**: Data permanently deleted within 30 days

### 5.3 Backups

- Backups retained for up to 90 days for disaster recovery

---

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

### 6.1 Access

- Request a copy of your personal data
- Export your data in machine-readable format (JSON)

### 6.2 Correction

- Update or correct your information at any time
- Access: Settings > Account > Edit Profile

### 6.3 Deletion

- Delete your account and all associated data
- Access: Settings > Account > Delete Account

### 6.4 Opt-Out

- Disable crash reports: Settings > Privacy > Crash Reports
- Disable analytics: Settings > Privacy > Usage Analytics
- Unsubscribe from emails: Click unsubscribe link in any email

### 6.5 Data Portability

- Export all your data: Settings > Account > Export Data
